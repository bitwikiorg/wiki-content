# V1 research-synthesis / knowledge-systems reconciliation

Issue #152 reconciliation for the single V1 `research_synthesis_knowledge_systems` source: `Knowledge Engine Patterns`.

## Source review

The historical document combines several different evidence classes:

- comparative observations about Wikipedia/Wikimedia-style hierarchy/navigation;
- descriptions of Grokpedia and H+pedia/RationalWiki-like systems, some with counts or architecture claims that were not accompanied by reproducible canonical evidence in the V1 source;
- Wolfram/entity-property-query design influence;
- three named BITwiki design hypotheses: '''Toroidal Feedback Loops''', '''Epistemic Stratification''', and '''Semantic Fractalization''';
- implementation recommendations that assumed obsolete V1 schema, numeric confidence and recursive-validation machinery.

The document therefore cannot be treated as an established architecture specification.

## Durable signal incorporated

The active `BITwiki:Comparative wiki research register` now explicitly preserves the useful V1 research lineage:

### Toroidal feedback / return paths
Retained as a '''navigation/research hypothesis''': a reader may traverse several domains and return to the starting subject with additional context.

Not adopted as an epistemic rule. A closed path, cross-domain agreement, or return loop does not make a claim true or raise its epistemic status automatically.

### Epistemic stratification
The durable idea has matured into current `BITwiki:Epistemic status`, `BITwiki:Evidence`, `BITwiki:Uncertainty`, and explicit confidence rationale.

Historical numeric confidence, five-tier/Axiomatic/Verified mechanics and automatic propagation are superseded.

### Semantic fractalization
Retained as a '''bounded design hypothesis''': similar organization/reuse patterns may recur at several scales.

Current Book Matter, Transclusion, recursive/focused portals and reading-depth surfaces are concrete bounded examples. BITwiki does not claim that its entire knowledge system is a mathematically or universally fractal architecture.

### External knowledge-system comparison
Wolfram/entity-property-query patterns remain comparative design evidence. External schemas or property systems are not imported merely because they appear powerful.

The wider V1 comparative-source families remain leads for research, but old counts, rankings, sitemap statistics, governance characterizations and implementation assertions require fresh primary-source verification before being reused as factual claims.

## Disposition

'''Knowledge Engine Patterns: incorporated as provenance-bearing research input, not as canonical architecture.'''

Its useful hypotheses and methodological lesson are now present in the active comparative-research register; its unverified comparative statistics and obsolete implementation prescriptions are explicitly not adopted.

## Result

The V1 research-synthesis/knowledge-systems family is content-reconciled for issue #152.

This does not itself authorize archive retirement.
