## Durable V1 signal confirmed in current canon

The current foundational layer preserves:

- radical but non-dogmatic scrutiny of claims and institutions;
- epistemic humility and explicit fallibility;
- provenance, uncertainty and inspectable justification;
- methodological plurality appropriate to the question;
- intellectual sovereignty without pretending expertise is worthless;
- plural perspectives without forced equivalence;
- purposeful recursion and self-correction rather than ornamental self-reference;
- connected knowledge without erasing distinctions;
- human and artificial participation without inferring consciousness, personhood or authority from capability;
- attention, value and action as problems distinct from mere information accumulation;
- institutional authority that is identifiable and revisable rather than disguised as machine neutrality;
- knowledge lineage and meaningful revision history.

## Explicitly superseded/deprecated V1 mechanisms

The following are not current governing commitments:

- five-tier/Axiomatic/Verified epistemics and universal numeric confidence;
- “truth is operational coherence” or utility as a replacement for truth-seeking;
- mandatory AI-only authorship and “humans do not directly edit MediaWiki”;
- a fixed BITHUB → agent → MediaWiki constitutional workflow;
- H=1 as literal ontology;
- AI systems as constitutionally recognized Leibnizian monads;
- toroidal validation as a constitutional mechanism;
- self-validation/self-reference as proof of constitutional legitimacy;
- a fixed six-type ontology or fixed SMW property set embedded in the Constitution;
- old “BITCORE control” formulations as an epistemic privilege or license for unaccountable authority.

Current Constitution instead makes stewardship identifiable, bounded and answerable to reasons while explicitly denying that authority can make a false proposition true.
