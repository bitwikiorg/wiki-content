## Durable V1 signal confirmed in current canon

The current epistemics layer preserves the strongest V1 ideas:

- evidence is claim-relative and method-dependent;
- sources and evidence are distinct;
- source prestige/consensus is not truth;
- independent replication and contradictory evidence matter;
- confidence requires reasons rather than decorative precision;
- uncertainty and disagreement should remain visible;
- provenance includes origin, transmission, transformation and review;
- claims can strengthen or weaken as evidence changes;
- historical or speculative artifacts can remain intellectually valuable without being promoted to established fact;
- AI-generated or AI-transformed material requires source checking and transformation provenance;
- audits exist for correction and learning, not punishment or quality-score theater.

## Explicitly deprecated/superseded V1 mechanisms

The following are not silently discarded; they are explicitly not current architecture:

- universal numeric confidence scores/ranges;
- a five-tier ladder ending in Verified/Axiomatic;
- treating Verified as a context-free epistemic status;
- mandatory claim subobjects for all claims;
- universal source-count thresholds;
- “epistemic committee” upgrade/downgrade governance as a canonical workflow;
- “truth as convergence” as BITwiki's operative definition of truth;
- L0–L3 comprehension/transformation/participation/emergence tests as epistemic validation;
- self-validation by a document's performative structure;
- universal EPV-style property naming as provenance/epistemic substrate.

## Result

All 16 V1 epistemics/evidence/provenance architecture sources are now content-reconciled for issue #152. Their useful non-deprecated substance is either present in current canon or their obsolete/conflicting mechanism is explicitly identified above.

This closes only this architecture family. It does not close issue #152 or authorize archive retirement.
